1. Environment

Environment means the surroundings in which living organisms live and interact. It includes both living and non-living components.

Components of Environment

  • Biotic Components: Plants, animals, microorganisms and humans.
  • Abiotic Components: Air, water, soil, sunlight, temperature and minerals.
Remember: Environment = Biotic + Abiotic components.

2. Ecosystem

An ecosystem is a functional unit in which living organisms interact with each other and with their physical environment.

Main Components

  • Producers
  • Consumers
  • Decomposers
  • Abiotic factors

Examples

  • Forest ecosystem
  • Pond ecosystem
  • Grassland ecosystem
  • Marine ecosystem

3. Natural Resources

Natural resources are materials and components obtained from nature that are useful to humans.

Types

  • Renewable Resources: Solar energy, wind, water and forests.
  • Non-Renewable Resources: Coal, petroleum, natural gas and minerals.
Renewable resources can naturally replenish, while non-renewable resources are limited and take a very long time to form.

4. Environmental Pollution

Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances into the environment that causes undesirable changes.

Types of Pollution

  • Air Pollution: Caused by smoke, harmful gases and industrial emissions.
  • Water Pollution: Caused by sewage, chemicals and industrial waste.
  • Soil Pollution: Caused by pesticides, plastics and solid waste.
  • Noise Pollution: Caused by traffic, machines and loud sounds.

5. Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature, rainfall and other weather patterns.

Major Causes

  • Burning of fossil fuels
  • Deforestation
  • Industrial activities
  • Increase in greenhouse gases

Effects

  • Rise in global temperature
  • Melting of glaciers
  • Sea-level rise
  • Changes in rainfall patterns
  • Loss of biodiversity

6. Global Warming

Global warming is the long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature mainly due to increasing greenhouse gases.

Major Greenhouse Gases

  • Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
  • Methane (CH₄)
  • Nitrous oxide (N₂O)
  • Water vapour

7. Sustainable Development

Sustainable development means development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

Three Main Pillars

  • Economic Development
  • Social Development
  • Environmental Protection
Simple Meaning: Development + Environment Protection + Future Generations.

8. Renewable Energy

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that are continuously replenished.

  • ☀️ Solar Energy
  • 🌬️ Wind Energy
  • 💧 Hydroelectric Energy
  • 🌱 Biomass Energy
  • 🌊 Tidal Energy
  • 🌋 Geothermal Energy

9. Biodiversity

Biodiversity means the variety of living organisms present on Earth.

Levels of Biodiversity

  • Genetic Diversity
  • Species Diversity
  • Ecosystem Diversity

Importance

  • Maintains ecological balance
  • Provides food and medicines
  • Supports ecosystems
  • Provides economic benefits

10. Waste Management

Waste management is the process of collecting, treating and disposing of waste in a safe and environmentally friendly way.

5R Principle

  • Refuse – Avoid unnecessary products.
  • Reduce – Reduce consumption.
  • Reuse – Use items again.
  • Recycle – Convert waste into useful materials.
  • Recover – Recover useful materials or energy from waste.

11. Role of Engineers in Sustainability

  • Develop energy-efficient technologies.
  • Design eco-friendly products.
  • Use renewable energy sources.
  • Reduce industrial pollution.
  • Develop sustainable infrastructure.
  • Promote recycling and waste management.
  • Use technology to conserve natural resources.
Engineer + Technology + Environment = Sustainable Future 🌍
