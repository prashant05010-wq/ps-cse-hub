Digital Electronics

Simple and exam-friendly digital electronics notes for CSE students.

1. What is Digital Electronics?

Digital electronics deals with electronic systems that use discrete values, usually represented by binary digits 0 and 1.

Digital system: Information is represented using binary values 0 and 1.

2. Number Systems

A number system is a method of representing numbers. Computers and digital systems commonly use different number systems.

  • Binary: Base 2
  • Octal: Base 8
  • Decimal: Base 10
  • Hexadecimal: Base 16
